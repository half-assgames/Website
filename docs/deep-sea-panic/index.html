Dive, grab, blast

Steer a tiny submarine through a mine-packed trench, grab sinking treasure, and turn the mines against each other with one well-timed pulse.

  • Drag to steer through drifting sea mines. One touch and your dive is over!
  • Grab sinking treasure for points and coins, and keep grabbing to build your streak.
  • Collect energy cells to charge your pulse, up to four charges.
  • Every blasted mine sets off the mines around it. Line them up for huge chain scores!
  • Skim past mines for CLOSE CALL bonuses.

Know your mines

Sea mines

Drift around and bounce off the walls.

Homing mines

Slowly hunt you down.

Shatter mines

Throb, flash and burst into three fast little mines, unless you defuse them first.

Every dive counts

  • Treasure banks coins between runs.
  • Unlock new subs in the shop.
  • Spend coins on boosts for your next dive: a head start of pulse charge, a free hit, double score, a treasure magnet or double coins.
  • Grab sinking crates for bonuses that stack for the whole run.

Fair and friendly

  • No ads. No in-app purchases. No timers or energy limits.
  • Plays offline.
  • Quick runs and instant restarts, built for "just one more dive".

How to play

On PC

Hold the left mouse button and your sub heads for the cursor. Space or right-click fires the pulse.

On a phone

Drag anywhere to steer. Tap the pulse button, or double-tap anywhere.
