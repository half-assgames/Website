Deep Sea Panic! Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Deep Sea Panic! does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress stays on your own device.

Who we are

Deep Sea Panic! is made by Half-Ass Games, an independent game studio in Australia. This policy covers every version of the game, including Android and PC.

What the game saves

To keep your progress, the game saves a small amount of game data on your device: your high score, coins, unlocked submarines, boosts bought in the shop, your settings (music, sound effects and brightness) and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-ins
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Other services

If you download the game from Google Play, Google handles the download, updates and your Google account under its own privacy policy. The game is built with the Unity engine. We have not turned on Unity's analytics or other online services in the game.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email Half-Ass Games at joshua@half-assgames.com.

About this website

half-assgames.com doesn't use cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and it never asks you for any information. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which records visitors' IP addresses for security under the GitHub privacy statement. The trailer on the Deep Sea Panic! page only loads from YouTube when you press play, and then Google's privacy policy applies.
